# ficx v9
New: Publish to all. One upload (Short/Reel, Normal video or Image), pick YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and ficx posts to each connected account. See SETUP-SOCIAL.md for the posting permissions.

## Modes and company workspaces
Pick how you work (header → Mode): **ficx Analytics** (tools only), **Hybrid** (analytics + CRM, all companies together) or **Company workspace** (everything for one company). Connections are shared: accounts you connected before creating a company can be reused by it ("Use the accounts I already connected"), or a company can have its own accounts. CRM records are tagged with the company and filtered when one is active; "All companies" (Hybrid) shows everything. Lead-capture webhook URLs shown inside a company include `?c=<companyId>` so leads land in that company.
