Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. What Blitz AI does

Blitz AI is a web service that lets you connect your own website and social media accounts (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Google and YouTube), see performance reports and improvement suggestions, and publish one piece of content to several platforms.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and name when you sign up or sign in.
  • Connected account data: when you connect a platform we receive an access token and read the information that platform allows: profile name and username, follower and following counts, your recent posts or videos with their captions and statistics (such as views, likes, comments, shares), and account insights.
  • Content you publish: files, captions and settings you submit when you use the publish feature.
  • Reports and technical data: the reports we generate for you and basic logs needed to run and secure the service.

3. How we use it

We use this information only to provide the service to you: to build your reports and suggestions, to publish content to the platforms you choose and only when you ask us to, and to keep the service secure. If you turn on AI-assisted analysis, captions and statistics from your reports are sent to Google's Gemini API to write the suggestions. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

Only with service providers that run Blitz AI (Google Firebase for sign-in and database, Render for hosting, Google Gemini for AI analysis), with the platform you ask us to publish to, and when the law requires it. Data from one platform is shown only to you.

5. Storage and retention

Access tokens, stored snapshots and reports are kept in our database until you disconnect the account or ask us to delete your data.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • In the dashboard open Connected social and click Disconnect on a platform. This deletes the stored access token and stored snapshot for that platform.
  • You can also remove Blitz AI's access in the settings of Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or your Google account at any time.
  • To delete your account and all data we hold about you, contact us (see below). We will act on the request within 30 days.

7. Children

Blitz AI is not intended for children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
