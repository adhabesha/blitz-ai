Questions, answered

Do I need a credit card?

No. The website check needs nothing, and the free starter account needs only an email or Google sign-in.

What happens in the demo?

About 20 minutes. You tell us what you want to fix first, we show how ficx does it with your kind of business, and we switch on full access if it is a fit. No pressure.

Which channels can I connect?

Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, WhatsApp, Telegram, Google and Gmail today. You connect your own accounts and can disconnect any time.

Will it replace my CRM?

It can. ficx has leads, contacts, companies, deals, tasks, teams and roles. You can import your existing leads from a CSV file.

Is cold outreach allowed?

ficx adds an unsubscribe link and your postal address to every outreach email and stops a sequence when someone replies or opts out. You are responsible for following the rules where you send.

Who is it for?

Small teams and agencies that win customers through conversations: real estate, clinics, local services, e-commerce and marketing agencies.
